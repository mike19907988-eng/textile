About Fulu Weaving

Tongling Fulu Weaving Co., Ltd. is a linen fabric manufacturer based in Tongling, Anhui — a historic flax production base in China. With in-house weaving and finishing, we supply pure linen, linen blend and home textile fabrics to customers in Europe, America, Japan and Southeast Asia.

From yarn to greige fabric to finished goods, our production is managed under one roof for consistent quality and reliable delivery.
